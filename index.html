About Otis Farm

Abuja's Trusted
Livestock Farm

We are a trusted livestock farm based in Abuja, Nigeria, supplying high‑quality poultry, fish, and farm animals to businesses, retailers, and bulk buyers since 2018.

Our focus on proper feeding, hygiene, and a consistent supply chain means you get the best every time. Bulk buyers get priority access, and early reservations are recommended due to high demand.

We believe in transparency and quality. From our farm to your table, every product is handled with care. Our team is dedicated to ensuring that you receive fresh, healthy livestock — exactly as ordered.
